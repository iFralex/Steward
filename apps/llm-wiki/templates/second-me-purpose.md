# Project Purpose — Second Me

## Goal

Build a reliable, searchable personal knowledge base about Alessio Antonucci: his work, education, projects, skills, professional history, relationships, commitments, decisions, and communications.

The wiki should help reconstruct context quickly, answer questions grounded in the source material, and support practical tasks such as preparing applications, recalling prior conversations, understanding project history, and following up on commitments.

## Key Questions

1. What has Alessio done, learned, built, decided, promised, or discussed, and when?
2. Which people, organizations, projects, opportunities, and events are connected?
3. What evidence supports each fact, and which source is the most recent or authoritative?
4. What open commitments, deadlines, applications, or follow-ups emerge from the material?
5. How have Alessio's experience, skills, interests, and professional narrative evolved over time?

## Scope

**In scope:**

- Email threads and their promoted attachments
- Current and historical CVs
- Cover letters, motivational letters, and reference letters
- Project descriptions, project history, technologies, roles, and outcomes
- Work, education, applications, interviews, collaborations, and professional relationships
- Events, deadlines, travel, administrative documents, and commitments when relevant to Alessio
- Explicitly supported biographical facts, preferences, goals, and decisions

**Out of scope:**

- Unsupported inference presented as fact
- Generic reference material that is not about Alessio or his activities
- Secrets, credentials, or authentication data
- Redundant pages that merely restate a source without adding retrieval value

## Operating Principles

- Prefer the most recent source when facts change over time, while preserving meaningful historical versions.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from plans, proposals, drafts, estimates, and third-party claims.
- Preserve dates, participants, organizations, project names, and source links.
- Merge repeated information into stable entity, project, experience, and topic pages.
- Treat email threads as evolving sources: later messages update the same conversation context.
- Surface contradictions and uncertainty explicitly rather than silently choosing one version.
- Keep private or sensitive information narrowly represented and only when useful for retrieval.

## Thesis

> Second Me is an evidence-backed working memory for Alessio: a connected account of his professional and personal context that remains traceable to original documents and communications.
